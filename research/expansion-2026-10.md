# Replated expansion, October 2026

Twelve recipes from eleven new restaurants (plus one for buccan). Each has a Simple and
a Chef version, The Restaurant Difference, sources, and confirmed / inferred / uncertain notes.

**How the research was done:** web search only. The restaurants' own websites, Wikimedia
and other image hosts were blocked by the research environment's network policy, so menu
wording comes from search-result snippets (every source note says so). No logo files or
food photographs were used. Each restaurant has an original typographic identity, and each
new recipe has an original line illustration labeled as such.
