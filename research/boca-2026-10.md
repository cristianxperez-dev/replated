# Boca (Cincinnati), October 2026

Five recipes from Boca, David Falk’s restaurant at 114 East Sixth Street, downtown Cincinnati.
Each has a Simple and a Chef version, The Restaurant Difference, sources, and confirmed / inferred /
uncertain notes.

**How the research was done:** web search only. bocacincinnati.com was blocked by the research
environment’s network policy, so menu wording comes from the owner’s copy of the official menu and
from search-result snippets of menus and diners’ reviews (every source note says so). The logos are
the restaurant’s own gold wordmark and navy tile, supplied by the owner. The line illustrations are
original.
